**[OURS, AND FLAGGED]** — the CHILD end of the age scale in `litmus.js` is **not** from Brysbaert.
Those anchors are the ordinary developmental range in the literature (Fenson's CDI norms and the
vocabulary-growth work following it), they **vary widely between studies**, and they are marked
PROVISIONAL per 071 in the file itself. It is a scale for reading growth on a bench, not a clinical
instrument, and the code says so rather than citing one paper for all of it.

## [OURS] — mechanisms with no source, named honestly

The credit ledger cuts both ways. These are **ours**, and none of them is a finding:

- **The Roe** as a seeded, deterministic genome for a mind — El-Fish's idea, applied to a mind
  rather than a fish.
- **The offers model** (196/197) — that an act offers several things, and the winning offer is the
  reason it was chosen. No source; it is a design.
- **The dictionary and the 402 marks** — Lonnie's own artwork and his own language.
- **The polysemy law** — one glyph, one name, many senses, with company or context selecting.
- **The kinship space** (221) — building a semantic space out of a dictionary, sheet layout and
  lived co-occurrence. The *method* is ordinary; that this particular space is his language's own
  structure is ours.
- **Trains of thought as implemented** (225) — Klinger gives the principle; the subject-holding,
  the three endings and the association-around-the-anchor are our construction.
- **Every PROVISIONAL magnitude** in `offers.js`, `learning.js`, `space.js`, `thinking.js`,
  `stories.js`, `interests.js`, `persona.js`, `vocabulary.js` — marked `[OURS, PROVISIONAL]` in
  place, and every one of them is a number Lonnie may move.
- **The measured thresholds** — the attention door at 0.67 (142) and curiosity's rouse at 0.371
  (201) are **measured**, not chosen, but the method of measuring-then-taking-the-middle is ours.
